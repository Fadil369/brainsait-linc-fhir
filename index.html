🚀 Overview

BrainSAIT is a production‑ready, edge‑computing AI platform that unites Cloudflare Workers AI with InterSystems IRIS. It delivers real‑time clinical reasoning, automated workflows, and patient‑centric insights — validated by a perfect 60/60 technical score and built for the Saudi healthcare ecosystem.

  • ✅ FHIR R4 Native — Full interoperability with modern EHR systems
  • ✅ Edge AI — Cloudflare Workers AI with zero cold-start latency
  • ✅ Bilingual Support — Arabic/English for Saudi healthcare
  • ✅ NPHIES Integration — Claims, eligibility, prior authorization
  • ✅ 21 AI Agents — 9 core + 12 specialized healthcare agents
  • ✅ Enterprise Security — HIPAA audit, RBAC, SMART on FHIR
